# ADOFAI prototype textures

These textures were extracted from the locally installed copy of **A Dance of
Fire and Ice** with AssetRipper 1.3.14 for this non-distributable Unity UI
prototype.

- `planet-red.png` — `planet-red`, Texture2D, 1024×93, 11 frames
- `planet-blue.png` — `planet-blue`, Texture2D, 1024×93, 11 frames
- `CoopTrail.png` — `CoopTrail`, Texture2D, 128×128

They are intentionally not wired into TUFReplay's AssetBundle or release
pipeline. Licensing and distribution must be decided before shipping them.
